# Archit's Growth Studio

Build a single-page freelance consulting website for Archit Aggarwal, an independent growth and performance marketing consultant who works with luxury, fashion, D2C, and lifestyle brands.

Overall feel: premium, editorial, and understated, closer to a luxury brand's site than a typical marketer's portfolio. Confident, not flashy. Keep the visual language typography and color-led rather than photo-heavy, avoid generic stock photography, and lean on whitespace, thin gold divider lines, and subtle geometric accents instead.

Design system:

- Colors: deep navy (#0B1F3A) as the primary dark tone, warm gold (#C4922A) as the accent, cream (#F9F6F0) as the light background, charcoal for body text

- Typography: a serif display font (Georgia or Playfair Display) for headings, a clean sans-serif (Inter) for body copy

- Generous whitespace, subtle scroll-triggered fade-ins

Sections, in order:

1. Hero — full-width navy section. Name: "Archit Aggarwal". Headline: "Growth Marketing Consultant for Luxury & D2C Brands." Subhead: "10+ years turning ad spend into store walk-ins, WhatsApp orders, and repeat customers for fashion, jewellery, and lifestyle brands." Two buttons: "Book a Call" and "See the Work" (scrolls to results).

2. About — short bio describing Archit as an "M-shaped marketer" with 10+ years across performance marketing, brand strategy, and growth, working across D2C, FMCG, luxury, exhibitions, F&B, and NGO sectors. Mention he currently leads brand positioning and performance marketing for a portfolio of 15+ luxury fashion, couture, and jewellery labels at a leading PR and brand management agency.

3. Services — 6 cards: Performance Marketing (Google & Meta Ads, full-funnel strategy), Brand Positioning & GTM Strategy, D2C & WhatsApp-First Commerce, Growth & Lead Generation Systems, SEO & Website Optimization, Marketing Analytics & Reporting.

4. Track Record — a stats grid, large numerals with a short label under each:

   - ₹0 to ₹65M ARR grown over 5 years

   - 30 to 900+ qualified leads a month within one quarter

   - 2.4X to 3.9X ROAS

   - 2.1X order volume growth via WhatsApp-first D2C

   - 34-38% increase in qualified store walk-ins

   - 50% reduction in cost per lead

5. Brands Worked With — a clean, evenly-spaced wordmark-style row of brand names (typography only, no logos needed): Wedding Asia, Studio Prive, La Polo, Self Storage India, Fur Ball Story, Monture, Adyaaye, Maharashtra Jewellers, Nike, Crocs, Kaya, Geetanjali, W for Women, Indriya.

6. Tools & Expertise — a tag-cloud row of tool names: Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, Google Analytics 4, SEMRush, Ahrefs, HubSpot CRM, Zapier, Figma, Google Looker Studio, Power BI.

7. Contact — simple contact form (name, email, message) plus direct links for email, phone, and LinkedIn. Closing line: "Let's talk about your next quarter."

Functionality: fully responsive, sticky nav with smooth-scroll links to each section, subtle fade/slide-in animations as sections enter the viewport, footer with contact links and copyright.
